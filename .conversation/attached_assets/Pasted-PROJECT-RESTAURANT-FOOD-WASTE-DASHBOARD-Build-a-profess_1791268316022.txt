PROJECT: RESTAURANT FOOD WASTE DASHBOARD

Build a professional, responsive web application called "Restaurant Food Waste Dashboard".

TAGLINE:
"Track. Analyze. Reduce."

PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Build a dashboard that helps restaurants track food waste and identify major sources of waste.

CORE PURPOSE:
The application should help restaurant owners and managers:
1. Record food waste.
2. Track the quantity of food wasted.
3. Track the estimated financial cost of wasted food.
4. Identify the major sources of food waste.
5. Identify the most wasted food categories and items.
6. Analyze waste trends.
7. Provide simple actionable recommendations to reduce waste.

IMPORTANT:
The application should not just display numbers. It must analyze the recorded data and clearly answer:
- What are we wasting?
- How much are we wasting?
- Why are we wasting it?
- Which food/category causes the most waste?
- How much money are we losing?
- What should the restaurant do to reduce the waste?

11. RECOMMENDATION ENGINE
==================================================

Do NOT use AI for recommendations.

Use simple JavaScript rule-based logic.

RULE 1:

If Overproduction is more than 30% of total waste:

Display:

"Recommendation:
Overproduction is your largest source of waste. Consider adjusting preparation quantities according to daily demand."

RULE 2:

If Spoilage is more than 25% of total waste:

Display:

"Recommendation:
A significant portion of waste is caused by spoilage. Review storage conditions and inventory rotation."

RULE 3:

If Customer Leftovers are more than 25% of total waste:

Display:

"Recommendation:
Customer leftovers are a major source of waste. Consider reviewing portion sizes and collecting customer feedback."

RULE 4:

If Expired Food is more than 15%:

Display:

"Recommendation:
Review inventory rotation and expiry monitoring to reduce expired food waste."

RULE 5:

If waste has increased significantly over recent days:

Display:

"Warning:
Food waste has increased recently. Review high-waste categories and daily preparation quantities."

RULE 6:

If waste is decreasing:

Display:

"Good progress:
Food waste is trending downward. Continue monitoring high-waste sources."

Recommendations must change dynamically based on actual data.

16. DATA CALCULATIONS
==================================================

TOTAL WASTE:

Calculate the sum of all waste quantities.

TOTAL COST:

Calculate the sum of all estimatedCost values.

WASTE EVENTS:

Count the number of waste records.

TOP WASTE SOURCE:

Group records by reason and find the reason with the highest total quantity.

TOP CATEGORY:

Group records by category and find the category with the highest total quantity.

MOST WASTED FOOD:

Group records by foodName and find the food with the highest total quantity.

PERCENTAGE:

For each waste source:

source waste / total waste * 100

All calculations should update automatically whenever records are added or deleted.
